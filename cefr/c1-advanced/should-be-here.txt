C1 (Advanced)

Focus: precision, nuance, stylistic control, flexibility across registers, and advanced syntax.

Typical C1 grammar topics:

1. Advanced Passive Structures

Reporting structures with passives (e.g., He is said to have…)

Passive with modals (e.g., should have been finished)

Get-passive nuance

2. Complex Subordinate Clauses

Concessive clauses (even though, whereas, while, in spite of)

Purpose clauses (so as to, in order that)

Result clauses (such…that, so…that)

3. Advanced Conditional Forms

Mixed conditionals

Conditional inversion

If it hadn’t been for, but for, hypothetical structures

4. Modality with Nuance

Modal perfect forms (might have, must have, shouldn’t have)

Modal meaning shifts (deduction, criticism, speculation, expectation)

5. Inversion for Emphasis

Never have I…

Rarely do we…

Only after…

Not until…

6. Complex Noun Phrases

Multiple modifiers

Nominalization

Expanded relative clauses

Reduced relative clauses

7. Reported Speech (Full System)

Reporting verbs with patterns (urge sb to, deny doing, warn sb not to)

Backshifting in complex contexts

Mixed reporting structures

8. Advanced Verb Patterns

Verb + object + infinitive/gerund variations

Perfect infinitives and perfect gerunds

Causatives (have/get something done)

9. Future Forms with Nuance

Future-in-the-past

Complex future reference in narratives

10. Emphatic and Stylistic Structures

Cleft sentences (What I found surprising was…)

Pseudo-clefts (The thing that bothers me is…)
