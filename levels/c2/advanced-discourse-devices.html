Introduction

Discourse devices connect ideas across sentences and paragraphs, guiding the reader through an argument. At C2, these go well beyond "however" and "therefore."

Explanation

Explanation

Register

notwithstanding, insofar as, and on which point belong to formal academic/legal writing; nevertheless and moreover are common in both formal writing and careful speech.

Grammar Rules

The Rules

a) Formal sentence-level connectors

  • notwithstanding (= despite, often placed after the noun it refers to, or at the start of a clause): The plan, criticism notwithstanding, went ahead. / Notwithstanding the criticism, the plan went ahead.
  • nevertheless / nonetheless (= despite what was just said): The evidence was weak. Nevertheless, the jury convicted him.
  • moreover / furthermore (= adding a stronger supporting point): The policy is expensive. Moreover, it has proven ineffective.
  • on which point / in this connection / to this end (= referring back to something just mentioned, moving the argument forward): The report calls for reform. On which point, several committee members disagreed.

b) Discourse markers structuring formal writing

  • that said / that having been said (= acknowledging a point before pivoting)
  • insofar as (= to the extent that): The plan is sound, insofar as it addresses short-term needs.
  • by the same token (= applying the same logic to a related point)
  • conversely (= introducing a contrasting point using parallel logic)

c) Cohesive devices across paragraphs — devices that create flow at a larger scale:

  • Resumptive/summative nouns referring back to a previous idea: this trend, such measures, this development, the aforementioned issue.
  • Signposting phrases that guide the reader through an argument's structure: it is worth noting that, this raises the question of, having established X, it follows that Y.

Examples

See It in Action

  • The proposal, controversy notwithstanding, was approved by a narrow margin.
  • The results were inconclusive. Nevertheless, the researchers pressed ahead with further trials.
  • The scheme is costly. Moreover, it fails to address the root cause of the problem.
  • The policy succeeded insofar as it reduced emissions, though it did little to boost the economy.
  • That said, not every critic dismissed the plan outright.
  • Conversely, smaller firms benefited significantly from the same regulation.
  • Such measures, however well-intentioned, often produce unintended consequences.
  • This development raises important questions about the future of the industry.
  • By the same token, if we accept this argument, we must also accept its implications.
  • It is worth noting that the study's sample size was relatively small.

Common Mistakes

Watch Out For

AvoidNotwithstanding of the criticism, they proceeded.

Use insteadNotwithstanding the criticism, they proceeded.

"Notwithstanding" is followed directly by a noun phrase — no preposition ("of") is needed.

AvoidThe evidence was weak, nevertheless the jury convicted him.

Use insteadThe evidence was weak. Nevertheless, the jury convicted him. (or with a semicolon: ...weak; nevertheless, the jury convicted him.)

"Nevertheless" is a discourse adverb, not a coordinating conjunction — it needs a full stop or semicolon before it, and typically a comma after.

AvoidThe plan worked insofar it addressed the budget.

Use insteadThe plan worked insofar as it addressed the budget.

The full connector is "insofar as," never "insofar" alone.

AvoidMoreover of the cost, the plan was poorly timed.

Use insteadMoreover, the plan was poorly timed. (as a separate point) — or: In addition to the cost, the plan was poorly timed.

"Moreover" doesn't take an object with "of" — it introduces a new independent point, not a phrase.

AvoidBy the same token, however, we should also consider...

Use insteadBy the same token, we should also consider... (choose one connector; don't stack contrasting ones)

"By the same token" extends the same logic; pairing it with "however" (which signals contrast) sends a contradictory signal.
