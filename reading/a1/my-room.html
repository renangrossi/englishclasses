Our Apartment

Three rooms, an old green sofa, and a window the sun comes through.

Play the audio and follow along, then read it again on your own. Tap or hover over a highlighted word to see what it means.

Our apartment is small, but we like it. There are three rooms: a bedroom, a living room, and a kitchen. The bathroom is next to the bedroom.

I like the living room best. There is a big window, and the afternoon sun comes in. Under it is an old green sofa from my grandmother.

Next to the sofa there is a small table with books. On the wall there is a picture of Ohio. The floor is wood and cold in the morning.

The kitchen is small — two people cannot cook in it together. There is a stove, a refrigerator, and a shelf for the plates.

In the bedroom we have a bed, a desk, and a chair. Jack works there every day. The window is on the street, so we hear the buses.

It is not big. But it is warm, it is near the park, and it is ours.

Key Vocabulary

  • next to at the side of
  • under below something
  • grandmother the mother of your mother or your father
  • wall the side of a room, where you put a picture
  • floor the part of a room you walk on
  • wood the hard material that comes from trees
  • stove the thing in a kitchen you cook on
  • shelf a flat board on a wall for putting things on
  • desk a table for working or writing
  • to hear to know a sound with your ears
  • warm a little hot, in a good way
  • ours it belongs to us
