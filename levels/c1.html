C1 — Advanced

Flexible, effective language use for academic and professional life.

ExercisesTest YourselfVocabularyReadingListeningWritingSpeakingRevision

Overview

C1 targets precision, nuance and stylistic control across registers: advanced passive structures, complex subordinate clauses, mixed conditionals, nuanced modality, inversion for emphasis, and the full reported-speech system.

C1

Exercises

The curriculum for C1 is planned around these 10 focus areas. Lessons are being written now.

Advanced Passive Structures

  • Reporting structures with passives (e.g., He is said to have…)
  • Passive with modals (e.g., should have been finished)
  • Get-passive nuance

Complex Subordinate Clauses

  • Concessive clauses (even though, whereas, while, in spite of)
  • Purpose clauses (so as to, in order that)
  • Result clauses (such…that, so…that)

Advanced Conditional Forms

  • Mixed conditionals
  • Conditional inversion
  • If it hadn’t been for, but for, hypothetical structures

Modality with Nuance

  • Modal perfect forms (might have, must have, shouldn’t have)
  • Modal meaning shifts (deduction, criticism, speculation, expectation)

Inversion for Emphasis

  • Never have I…
  • Rarely do we…
  • Only after…
  • Not until…

Complex Noun Phrases

  • Multiple modifiers
  • Nominalization
  • Expanded relative clauses
  • Reduced relative clauses

Reported Speech (Full System)

  • Reporting verbs with patterns (urge sb to, deny doing, warn sb not to)
  • Backshifting in complex contexts
  • Mixed reporting structures

Advanced Verb Patterns

  • Verb + object + infinitive/gerund variations
  • Perfect infinitives and perfect gerunds
  • Causatives (have/get something done)

Future Forms with Nuance

  • Future-in-the-past
  • Complex future reference in narratives

Emphatic and Stylistic Structures

  • Cleft sentences (What I found surprising was…)
  • Pseudo-clefts (The thing that bothers me is…)
