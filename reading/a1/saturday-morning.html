A1 · Everyday Life

Saturday Morning

No alarm, no shop, no bus. Emma's one slow morning of the week.

ReadingPracticeDiscussion

Reading

Play the audio and follow along, then read it again on your own. Tap or hover over a highlighted word to see what it means.

On Saturday I don't work, so I don't put the alarm on. I sleep in until nine. It is the best morning of the week.

Jack gets up first. When I come into the kitchen there is coffee, and the window is open.

We have a slow breakfast — eggs, bread, fruit. We don't talk much. We don't have to.

Then we clean the apartment. I wash the plates and Jack does the laundry. It takes an hour.

After that we go out. If it is warm we take a walk in the park near our building. If it rains we stay home and read.

At one o'clock Sam comes down from upstairs with his dog. We talk in front of the building for ten minutes.

In the afternoon we do nothing, and that is the point. On Monday I get up at five again.

Key Vocabulary

  • alarm the clock that makes a noise to wake you up
  • to sleep in to stay in bed later than usual
  • slow not fast; without hurrying
  • much a lot
  • to have to to need to do something
  • to wash to clean with water
  • laundry the clothes you wash
  • to take a walk to walk somewhere for pleasure
  • to stay to not leave a place
  • nothing not one thing
  • the point the reason why something is good or important
