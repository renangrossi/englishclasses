Your Data

Privacy

What this course stores about you, where it is kept, and how to delete it. The short version: you can use everything here without an account, and if you do sign in, your details are used only to save your progress.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who runs this site

Renan the Teacher is a free English course run by one teacher, Renan Grossi. For anything about your data, write to renanvg@proton.me.

If you do not sign in

Your XP, streak, badges, exercise results and review history are kept only in your own browser, on your own device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data, or pressing Reset my progress on the My Progress page, deletes them.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you choose to, we receive from Google and store:

  • your name, your email address and your Google profile picture;
  • your course progress: XP, streak, badges, which exercises you have done and how you scored, and your review history.

We use this for one thing only: to save your progress so that it is there when you open the course on another phone or computer. We never see your Google password, and we do not ask Google for access to your email, contacts, files or anything else.

We do not sell this information, share it with anyone, use it for advertising, or send you any email except the sign-in emails you ask for. Each student can read and change only their own progress; your teacher can see it in order to run the course.

If you sign in with your email

You can also sign in without Google: type your email address and we send you a sign-in link and code. We then store only your email address and the same course progress as above — no name and no picture. To deliver that email, your address is passed to Brevo, the email delivery service this site uses, which sends the sign-in message and nothing else.

Where it is kept

Accounts and saved progress are stored with Supabase, the database service this site uses. Google sign-in is handled by Google, and sign-in emails are sent by Brevo. We keep your account and progress until you delete them or ask us to.

How to delete your data

  • Your progress: while signed in, press Reset my progress on the My Progress page. This erases the progress saved in your account, on every device.
  • Your whole account (name, email and progress): write to renanvg@proton.me from the address you signed in with, and it will be deleted.
  • You can also remove this site's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings.

You can also write to the same address to ask for a copy of what is stored about you, or to have it corrected.

Other things the site does

  • AI Teacher. A question you type to the AI Teacher is sent, with the current conversation, through our server at Cloudflare to Groq, the company whose AI model writes the answer. We do not store the conversation. A random identifier with no personal information is kept for up to 24 hours to limit how many questions one browser can ask per day. Groq may log requests to prevent abuse, so please do not type personal information there.
  • Quick dictionary. A word you look up is sent to a public dictionary service (dictionaryapi.dev or Wiktionary) to fetch its definition.
  • Hosting and fonts. The pages are served by GitHub Pages and the fonts by Google Fonts. Like any web server, they receive your IP address when your browser loads a page.
  • Browser storage. The site keeps your light or dark theme, your progress and, if you sign in, your sign-in session in your browser's storage. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no analytics.

Children

If you are under the age at which you can open your own Google account in your country, please use the course without signing in, or ask a parent or guardian first.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
