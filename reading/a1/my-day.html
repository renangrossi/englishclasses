A Tuesday

One ordinary day, from five in the morning to ten at night.

Play the audio and follow along, then read it again on your own. Tap or hover over a highlighted word to see what it means.

I get up at five. I take a shower, I make coffee, and I leave the apartment at half past five. Jack is still in bed.

I start work at six. The first hour is quiet, and then the shop is busy until ten. I like the busy hours, because the time goes fast.

At half past twelve I have lunch. I eat a sandwich and I sit down for thirty minutes.

I finish at two and I am home at three. I am tired, but the apartment is quiet. I read on the green sofa, or I call my mother in Ohio.

Jack finishes at six. Today it is my turn to cook, so we have chicken and rice. We eat together and we talk about the day.

After dinner we watch TV. At ten I go to bed. Tomorrow is the same, and that's okay.

Key Vocabulary

  • to get up to leave your bed in the morning
  • still continuing now; not finished
  • busy with a lot of work or a lot of people
  • to go fast to pass quickly
  • to sit down to put your body on a chair
  • tired when you need to rest or sleep
  • to call to telephone somebody
  • my turn the time when it is my job to do something
  • to watch to look at something for a time
  • that's okay it is not a problem
