C2 (Proficient)

Focus: mastery, stylistic manipulation, native-like control, and near-academic fluency.

Typical C2 grammar topics:

1. Full Command of All Tenses and Aspects

Precise, subtle contrasts (e.g., habitual past in narrative, iterative aspect)

Shifts in tense for rhetorical effect

2. Highly Complex Sentence Architecture

Multiple subordinate clauses nested in long sentences

Multi-clause fronting

High-density noun phrases and academic phrasing

3. Advanced Discourse Devices

Sentence-level connectors (notwithstanding, nevertheless, moreover, on which point)

Discourse markers in formal writing

Cohesive devices across paragraphs

4. Stylistic Transformations

Active → passive rephrasing at high nuance

Multiple ways to re-express the same idea with tone changes

Grammar-driven style shifts (formal, informal, legal, academic, rhetorical)

5. Complex Ellipsis and Substitution

Ellipsis in reduced clauses

Omission patterns common in academic and literary writing

Pro-forms in complex texts

6. High-Level Clefts and Inversions

Literary inversion

Multiple pseudo-clefts across clauses

Fronted modifiers for stylistic impact

7. Native-Like Use of Modality and Attitude

Subtle modal shading (tentativeness, concession, politeness, epistemic stance)

Layered modality (He might have been expected to have been informed)

8. Rare or Literary Structures

Advanced subjunctive (formal or archaic)

Emphatic do in literary contexts

Inverted conditionals with omitted auxiliary

9. Mastery of Register

Grammar choices aligned with academic, legal, journalistic, poetic, casual, and technical styles

Recasting sentences to match specific genres

10. Near-Native Transformation Skills

Rewriting entire paragraphs while preserving meaning and structure

Handling ambiguity and pragmatic nuance through grammar
