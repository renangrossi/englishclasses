A Day Hiking in the Mountains

A simple past-tense narrative about one long, good day outdoors.

Play the audio and follow along, then read it again on your own. Tap or hover over a highlighted word to see what it means.

Last weekend, I went hiking with my friends. We all love being outdoors, so we picked a trail up in the mountains. The weather was perfect — sunny, but not too hot.

We started early in the morning. The trail was quiet and we could hear birds singing. The path went through a forest of tall trees. The air smelled fresh, and the leaves under our feet made a soft sound as we walked.

After an hour, the trail got steeper. We climbed up a long hill. It was hard work, and we stopped to help each other more than once. At the top we rested, and the view was worth it: we could see the whole valley below us, with green fields and a narrow river running through them.

We sat down and ate our lunch there. We had brought sandwiches, apples and plenty of water. Food always tastes better outside. We talked and laughed and stayed longer than we planned.

After lunch we kept going. The path followed a stream for a while. The water was so clear that we could see small fish swimming near the rocks. We crossed a wooden bridge and stopped to take pictures. The sound of the water was wonderfully relaxing.

By the middle of the afternoon we reached a lake. It was wide and deep blue, with mountains standing all around it. Two of us went in for a swim; the rest of us sat on the shore and watched. The water was cold, but after so much walking it was refreshing.

The hike back was easier, because it was downhill almost the whole way. We were tired, but we were happy. The sun was going down and the sky turned orange and pink behind the peaks.

Hiking is more than just walking. It is about being outside, seeing somewhere new, and spending a whole day with people you like. It makes me feel alive, and I cannot wait to go again.

Key Vocabulary

  • a trail a path for walking, usually in nature
  • steep rising sharply; hard to climb
  • a valley the low land between two hills or mountains
  • a stream a small river
  • the shore the land at the edge of a lake or sea
  • downhill going down a slope (opposite: uphill)
  • a peak the pointed top of a mountain
  • refreshing making you feel cool and full of energy again

Speaking & Writing

Discussion

There are no right answers here — use these to practice speaking, or write a short answer to each.

  • Describe a day out in nature that you remember. Where did you go, and who went with you?
  • The writer says food always tastes better outside. Do you agree? Why?
  • Would you rather spend a free day in the mountains, at the beach, or in a city? Explain your choice.
  • Write about 70 words describing a walk you took recently, using the simple past.
