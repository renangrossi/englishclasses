Let's learn

Pre-A1 — Survival English

For absolute beginners. No prior English knowledge is assumed — start here if you're not sure where to begin.

Overview

Pre-A1 does not assume you already know how English works. It covers the alphabet and sounds, greetings, numbers and time, the instructions used throughout this website, everyday vocabulary, and the handful of sentence patterns (I am / I have / I like / I want, and the verb "to be") that let you start communicating immediately. Finish these 8 lessons, in order, before moving on to A1 — Beginner.

Pre-A1

Lessons

8 lessons, in order — each one builds on the one before it.

I

The Alphabet & Sounds

Learn the 26 letters of English and how to spell your name out loud.

II

Greetings & Introductions

Say hello, introduce yourself, and ask how someone is.

III

Numbers, Time & Dates

Count from 0 to 100, tell the time, and say the days and months.

IV

Classroom & Study Language

The words you'll see on this website, in every lesson, from now on.

V

People & Everyday Objects

Name the people around you, colors, and common objects.

VI

I Am / I Have / I Like / I Want

The four sentence patterns that let you say the most, with the least grammar.

VII

Subject Pronouns & To Be — A Slow Start

The words for I, you, he, she — and the three forms of the most important verb in English.

VIII

Survival Reading & Listening

Recognize the signs and short words you'll see in real life, and prepare your ear for real English.

Pre-A1

Listening

Four very short recordings, read slowly and clearly, so your ear can start recognizing English sounds before you know much grammar at all.

You don't need every word. At this stage, understanding the general idea is a real win. Listen once without reading, then open the transcript and listen again — the words you missed will start to jump out.

Pre-A1

Guided Listening Practice

Short recordings paired with texts from this level, read slightly slower than natural speed. Listen first — try to answer before opening the transcript.

What's Next

After Pre-A1

Once you're comfortable with all 8 lessons above, move on to A1 — Beginner, where you'll start building full sentences: negatives, questions, plurals, and much more.
