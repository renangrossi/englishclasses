Introduction

Grammar Rules

The Rules

a) Reporting verb patterns — many verbs have their own fixed structure:

  • verb + object + to-infinitive: urge/persuade/encourage/warn + sb + to do sth "You should see a doctor," she said. → She urged him to see a doctor.
  • verb + gerund (often with a preposition): deny doing, admit doing, insist on doing, apologize for doing "I didn't take it," he said. → He denied taking it.
  • verb + object + not + to-infinitive: warn/tell/advise + sb + not + to do sth "Don't touch that," he said. → He warned her not to touch it.
  • verb + that-clause: suggest/insist/recommend + that + subject + (should) + bare infinitive "Let's leave early," she said. → She suggested that they (should) leave early.

b) Backshifting in complex contexts — when the reporting verb is in the past, tenses generally shift one step back (present → past, past → past perfect, will → would), BUT:

  • No backshift is needed for general truths/facts: "The Earth orbits the Sun," he said. → He said that the Earth orbits the Sun.
  • No backshift if the situation is still true/relevant now: "I'm working in Berlin," she said (and still is). → She said she's working in Berlin.
  • Modals like "would," "could," "might," "should," "ought to" don't change.

c) Mixed reporting structures — combining reported statements, questions and commands in one passage, and reporting entire exchanges naturally rather than word-for-word.

Examples

See It in Action

  • She urged him to reconsider his decision.
  • He denied breaking the vase.
  • They warned us not to go near the cliff edge.
  • The doctor recommended that she rest for a week.
  • He apologized for being late.
  • She insisted on paying for dinner.
  • The manager suggested that the meeting be postponed.
  • He admitted taking the last cookie.
  • She promised to call as soon as she landed.
  • They accused him of lying to the committee.
  • The teacher advised the students not to panic during the exam.
  • He said that water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

Common Mistakes

Watch Out For

AvoidShe suggested him to leave early.

Use insteadShe suggested that he (should) leave early. / She suggested leaving early.

"Suggest" never takes object + to-infinitive; use "suggest that..." or "suggest + gerund."

AvoidHe denied to steal the money.

Use insteadHe denied stealing the money.

"Deny" is followed by a gerund, not a to-infinitive.

AvoidShe warned him don't go outside.

Use insteadShe warned him not to go outside.

"Warn" needs object + not + to-infinitive, not a direct quote structure.

AvoidHe said me that he was tired.

Use insteadHe told me that he was tired. / He said (that) he was tired.

"Say" doesn't take a direct object for the listener; use "tell" for that, or drop the object with "say."

AvoidShe said that the Earth orbited the Sun.

Use insteadShe said that the Earth orbits the Sun.

General/scientific truths usually keep the present tense even after a past reporting verb.
