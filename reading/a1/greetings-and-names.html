A New Neighbor

Emma works in a coffee shop in Chicago. This morning somebody new comes in.

Play the audio and follow along, then read it again on your own. Tap or hover over a highlighted word to see what it means.

My name is Emma. I live in Chicago with my husband, Jack. We have a small apartment near a park, and I work at a coffee shop on the same street.

This morning a man comes in. I don't know him. He looks tired.

“Good morning,” I say. “What can I get you?”

“Coffee, please. Black.” He smiles. “I'm Sam. I live upstairs — I moved in yesterday.”

“Nice to meet you, Sam. I'm Emma. Are you from Chicago?”

“No, I'm from Texas. Everything here is new for me — the city, the people, the weather.”

“I'm from Ohio,” I say. “But I live here now. Ten years.”

I give him his coffee. “Here you go. It's on me today. Welcome to the building.”

“Thank you,” he says. “See you later, Emma.”

Key Vocabulary

  • near close to something, not far
  • the same not different
  • tired when you need to rest or sleep
  • to smile to make a happy face
  • to move in to come to live in a new home
  • upstairs on a higher floor of a building
  • Nice to meet you what you say when you meet somebody the first time
  • Here you go what you say when you give somebody something
  • It's on me I pay for it, not you
  • building a place with apartments or offices inside it
  • See you later goodbye, until the next time
