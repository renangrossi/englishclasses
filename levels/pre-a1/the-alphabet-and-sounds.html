PRE-A1

The Alphabet & Sounds

Learn the 26 letters of English and how to spell your name out loud.

ObjectivesRulesExamplesPracticeSummary

Introduction

English has 26 letters. Some sound similar to Portuguese, and some are very different. Listen to each letter, then practice saying it.

By the end of this lesson you can…

  • Say the 26 letters of the English alphabet
  • Spell your own name out loud, letter by letter
  • Understand simple classroom questions like "How do you spell that?"

Spelling out loud

To spell a word, say each letter separately.

  • How do you spell your name? — M-A-R-I-A. (M, A, R, I, A)
  • How do you spell "cat"? — C-A-T.

For a double letter, say "double": B-A-double L (ball).

Examples

See It in Action

  • A, B, C, D, E, F, G...
  • How do you spell your name?
  • My name is Ana. A-N-A.
  • Can you spell that, please?
  • It's spelled T-E-A-C-H-E-R.
