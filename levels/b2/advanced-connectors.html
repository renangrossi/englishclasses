B2

Advanced Connectors

Formal, written-style connectors for contrast, addition, and consequence.

ObjectivesRulesExamplesCommon MistakesPracticeSummaryTest Yourself

Introduction

You already know but/so/because. This lesson gives you the more formal connectors used in essays, reports, and professional writing.

By the end of this lesson you can…

  • Use whereas and despite/in spite of for formal contrast
  • Use furthermore/moreover for formal addition
  • Use nevertheless/consequently for formal consequence and contrast

Grammar Rules

The Rules

a) Contrast

  • whereas (neutral contrast, joining two clauses): The north is mountainous, whereas the south is flat.
  • despite / in spite of + noun/gerund: Despite the rain, we went out. / In spite of losing, they stayed positive.
  • nevertheless (starts a new sentence): The plan was risky. Nevertheless, they went ahead.

b) Addition

  • furthermore / moreover (adds a stronger point, formal, starts a new sentence): The product is expensive. Furthermore, it's poorly made.

c) Consequence

  • consequently / as a result (formal result, starts a new sentence): Sales fell sharply. Consequently, the company cut costs.

Examples

See It in Action

  • The north is mountainous, whereas the south is flat.
  • Despite the rain, we went out.
  • The plan was risky. Nevertheless, they went ahead.
  • The product is expensive. Furthermore, it's poorly made.
  • Sales fell sharply. Consequently, the company cut costs.
  • In spite of losing, the team stayed positive.

Common Mistakes

Watch Out For

AvoidDespite it was raining, we went out.

Use insteadDespite the fact that it was raining, we went out. / Despite the rain, we went out.

"Despite" takes a noun or gerund, not a full clause directly.

AvoidThe plan was risky, nevertheless they went ahead.

Use insteadThe plan was risky. Nevertheless, they went ahead.

"Nevertheless" typically starts a new sentence, with a period before it, not a comma joining one sentence.

AvoidThe report is late, furthermore is incomplete.

Use insteadThe report is late. Furthermore, it is incomplete.

"Furthermore" needs its own clause with a subject ("it"), not a fragment.

Summary

Review

  • Whereas = neutral contrast joining two clauses. Despite/in spite of + noun/gerund = contrast.
