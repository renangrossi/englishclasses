Five in the Morning

Emma starts work before the city wakes up. This is her day, hour by hour.

Play the audio and follow along, then read it again on your own. Tap or hover over a highlighted word to see what it means.

I start work early. The coffee shop opens at six in the morning, so I get up at five.

I don't like five o'clock. But I get up, I take a shower, and I make coffee at home. Jack is still in bed. He works from home, and he starts at nine.

I leave the apartment at half past five. The bus comes at 5:40 and takes twenty minutes.

The first hour is quiet. Then, at seven, the shop is busy. Everybody wants coffee before work, and from seven to ten I don't stop.

I have lunch at half past twelve, but only for thirty minutes. I finish at two in the afternoon and I am home at three.

Jack finishes at six. We have dinner together at seven, and I go to bed at ten. Tomorrow I get up at five again.

Key Vocabulary

  • early before the usual time
  • to get up to leave your bed in the morning
  • to take a shower to wash your body under water
  • still continuing now; not finished
  • to leave to go away from a place
  • half past thirty minutes after the hour
  • to take to need an amount of time
  • quiet with little noise
  • busy with a lot of work or a lot of people
  • to finish to end; to stop doing something
  • together with each other
